# Welcome

This site is an attempt to provide a unified, easy to access documentation system for UAVs@Berkeley. Documentation pages are written in [Markdown](https://zensical.org/docs/authoring/markdown/) and support [MathJax](https://zensical.org/docs/authoring/math/).


## Who we are

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles at Berkeley is an engineering Registered Student Organization sponsored by the Associated Students of the University of California and the Engineering Student Council at UC Berkeley. We are the premier student organization for all things unmanned, including competition, research, and flying for fun. We have a spiffy lab in 107A McLaughlin Hall and are open to all students, regardless of engineering background.
