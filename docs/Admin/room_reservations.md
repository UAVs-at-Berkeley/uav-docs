## Civil Engineering MOU

## Eshleman/bNorth Conference Rooms

"Express rooms". More info at [Berkeley Event Services](https://eventservices.berkeley.edu/express-room-availability/).

=== "Rooms"

    - bNorth: 82A (16), 82D (36)
    - Eshleman: 250 (8), 310 (12), 318 (6), 338 (10), 342 (6), 350 (8)

=== "Booking"

    Book via [25Live](https://25live.collegenet.com/pro/berkeley) (Signatories only). Same-day booking OK.

=== "Cancellation"

    Email [eventservices@berkeley.edu](eventservices@berkeley.edu) up to reservation start time

=== "Restrictions"

    - Max 3 hours per day
    - Max 16 future reservations at a time
    - Not available during RRR and finals weeks, academic and administrative holidays, and summer
    - Cannot book until 7 days before start of semester

## General Assignment Classrooms

More info at [Berkeley Event Services](https://eventservices.berkeley.edu/classroom-reservations/).

=== "Rooms"

    Many shapes and sizes!

=== "Booking"

    Book via [25Live](https://25live.collegenet.com/pro/berkeley) (Signatories only).

    - Weekday reservations a minimum of 7 business days in advance for single classroom, 21 days in advance of multiple classrooms in 1 day
    - Weekend reservations a minimum of 14 business days in advance for single classroom, 21 days in advance of multiple classrooms in 1 day

=== "Cancellation"

    Email [classroomevents@berkeley.edu](classroomevents@berkeley.edu)

    - Weekday reservations a minimum of 3 business days in advance
    - Weekend reservations a minimum of 14 business days in advance

=== "Restrictions"

    - No reservations before 8pm for first 4 weeks of instruction
    - Not available during RRR and finals weeks, academic and administrative holidays, and summer
    - Weekday reservations booking opens July 1st for fall semester and December 1st for spring semester
    - Weekend reservations booking available up to 365 days in advance
    - No food or drink allowed
